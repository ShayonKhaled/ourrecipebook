## Ingredients

- Dough
- ▢ 230 g lukewarm milk (1 cup)
- ▢ 2 eggs, room temperature
- ▢ 75 g unsalted butter, soft or melted (⅓ cup)
- ▢ 50 g granulated sugar (¼ cup)
- ▢ 540 g bread flour (4¼ cups)
- ▢ 10 g salt (1¾ tsp)
- ▢ 7 g fast action yeast (2¼ tsp / 1 packet)
- Garlic Butter Filling
- ▢ 140 g very soft unsalted butter (10 tbsp)
- ▢ 30–40 g garlic, finely grated (6–8 cloves / 2–3 tbsp)
- ▢ 5 g salt (1 tsp)
- ▢ 8 g parsley (2 tbsp)
- ▢ Pinch chili flakes (optional)
- Cheese Filling
- ▢ 250 g mozzarella, shredded (2 cups)
- ▢ 120 g cheddar, shredded (1 cup)
- ▢ 35–50 g parmesan (⅓–½ cup)
- Egg Wash
- ▢ 1 egg
- ▢ 1 tsp milk or cream
- Butter Herb Glaze
- ▢ 45 g melted butter (3 tbsp)
- ▢ 1 tbsp parsley
- ▢ Pinch of salt (if using unsalted butter)

## Method

1. Mix everything well ^^
