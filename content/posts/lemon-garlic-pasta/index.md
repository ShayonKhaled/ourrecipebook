The pasta we make when it is already late and nobody wants to think. It comes
together in the time it takes the water to boil, and it is sharp enough to wake
you up.

## Ingredients

- 400 g spaghetti or linguine
- 6 cloves garlic, sliced thin
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 80 ml olive oil
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 60 g parmesan, finely grated
- A large handful of flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and salt it hard - it should
   taste like the sea.
2. Drop the pasta in and set a timer for one minute less than the packet says.
3. While it cooks, warm the olive oil in a wide pan over low heat. Add the
   garlic and chilli flakes and cook gently for 3-4 minutes. You want the garlic
   pale gold and smelling sweet, never brown.
4. Add the lemon zest and stir for 30 seconds.
5. Reserve a mug of the pasta water, then drain the pasta and tip it straight
   into the pan.
6. Add the lemon juice and a splash of the pasta water. Toss hard for a minute
   until the sauce turns glossy and clings to the strands.
7. Off the heat, stir through the parmesan and parsley. Season and serve
   immediately.

## Notes

- If the sauce looks thin, keep tossing over the heat; the starch in the water
  tightens it up.
- The parmesan goes in off the heat. On the heat it clumps.
- A spoonful of the pasta water left in the pan is worth more than extra oil.
