The topping is deliberately heavy on the butter and light on the sugar, so it
bakes into something closer to a shortbread lid than a dusting of crumbs. It
works with almost any berry, fresh or frozen.

## Ingredients

### For the fruit

- 600 g blueberries (frozen is fine, do not thaw)
- 2 tbsp caster sugar
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 1 tsp lemon juice

### For the crumble

- 200 g plain flour
- 100 g cold butter, cubed
- 60 g caster sugar
- 40 g rolled oats
- 1 pinch of salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 190C.
2. Toss the blueberries with the sugar, cornflour and lemon juice, then tip
   them into a baking dish. The cornflour is what stops the base turning into
   soup.
3. Rub the cold butter into the flour with your fingertips until it looks like
   coarse breadcrumbs. Work quickly and keep some lumps.
4. Stir in the sugar, oats and salt.
5. Scatter the crumble over the fruit in an even layer. Do not press it down -
   pressed crumble goes dense.
6. Bake for 40-45 minutes until the top is golden and the fruit is bubbling at
   the edges.
7. Rest for 15 minutes before serving. It is molten straight from the oven.

## Notes

- From frozen, add 5-10 minutes to the bake.
- A dish roughly 20 x 25 cm gives the right ratio of topping to fruit.
- Leftovers are good cold for breakfast, which is not a mistake.
