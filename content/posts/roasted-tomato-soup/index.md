Roasting the tomatoes first is the whole trick. It takes the soup from sharp and
watery to something deep and sweet, and the oven does the work while you get on
with something else.

## Ingredients

- 1.2 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
- 1 onion, cut into wedges
- 1 head of garlic, top sliced off
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 100 ml cream (optional)
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200C and line a large tray.
2. Spread the tomatoes and onion cut-side up. Sit the garlic head in a corner.
   Drizzle everything with the olive oil, scatter the oregano, and season well.
3. Roast for 35-40 minutes until the tomato edges are catching and the onion is
   soft and browned.
4. Squeeze the garlic cloves out of their skins into a pot. Add the roasted
   tomatoes and onion, then the stock.
5. Simmer for 10 minutes, then blend until smooth. An upright blender gives a
   silkier result than a stick blender.
6. Stir in the balsamic vinegar, then the cream if using. Taste and adjust the
   salt - roasted tomatoes need more than you expect.
7. Serve with something to dunk.

## Notes

- Out of season tomatoes are fine here; roasting rescues them.
- The soup thickens a lot overnight. Loosen it with stock when reheating.
- Freezes well for about three months.
