Second end-to-end test, checking that the site actually redeploys.

## Ingredients

- 3 ripe bananas
- 200 g plain flour
- 100 g sugar

## Method

1. Mash the bananas.
2. Mix everything and pour into a tin.
3. Bake at 180C for 50 minutes.
