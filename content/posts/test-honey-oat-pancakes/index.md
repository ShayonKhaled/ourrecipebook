## Ingredients

- 150 g rolled oats
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp baking powder
- A pinch of salt

## Method

1. Whisk the eggs and honey together.
2. Stir in the oats, baking powder and salt and let it sit for 10 minutes.
3. Fry in spoonfuls over a medium heat until golden on both sides.

## Notes

  The batter thickens as it sits, so loosen it with a splash of milk if needed.
