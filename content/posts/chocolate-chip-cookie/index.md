It's a fucking chocolate chip cookie

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup granulated white sugar
- 1 cup of light brown sugar
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 eggs
- 3 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp baking power
- 1 tsp seasalt
- A bunch of chocolate pieces

## Method

1. Cream butter and sugar, till it turns a lighter shade and is soft and risen in volume. Add in eggs and fluff it up. Sieve in flour and the other nonsense (cept chocolate). It will turn into cookie dough. Add in chocolate and fold it in.
2. Leave in fridge for a bit and then bake at 180 degrees Celsius convection for 9 minutes, then at 180 degrees micro-convection for 2 minutes. Be very careful to watch it for those last two minutes. If the butter starts sizzling and the cookie shape doesn't change anymore, it's becoming overbaked and hard. Pause before that happens. Cookies should be soft and shapeable after you bring them out of the oven . You can shape them up by shaking them around a circular mold.

## Notes

  I recommend using spatula to mix after adding flour. Don't use hands coz that's sticky and don't use mixer coz that's a waste. The batter should not be super clumpy, though it might be a little clumpy.
