This is a small home recipe book - the things we cook often enough to stop
looking up.

Each recipe is one folder under `content/posts/`, holding the write-up and its
photo, so a recipe and its image always travel together.

## How the pages are built

The site runs on [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) with the
[Coyote theme](https://github.com/nightswinger/hugo-theme-coyote), which renders
each recipe as a card on the home page and gives us tag pages plus Fuse.js
search for free.

The photo in each card is a placeholder colour card for now; drop a real
`image.jpeg` into the recipe's folder and it takes over.
