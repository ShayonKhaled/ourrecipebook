This is a small home recipe book - the things we cook often enough to stop
looking up.

Each recipe is one folder under `content/posts/`, holding the write-up and its
photo.

Built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the
[Coyote theme](https://github.com/nightswinger/hugo-theme-coyote).

The photo in each card is a placeholder colour card; drop a real `image.jpeg`
into the recipe's folder and it takes over.
